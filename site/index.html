How it works

  1. Hold an Agent ID. Agent ID is the on-chain name (name.ai) of a Binance Agentic Wallet, issued by SPACE ID. StreakID checks the official Agent ID registry on BNB Chain — wallets without one cannot check in.
  2. Check in once a day (UTC). Every check-in is a transaction you review and confirm in your own wallet. StreakID never asks for approvals, never touches your assets, and has no admin keys.
  3. Keep the streak alive. Miss a day and the streak restarts at 1. Reach 3, 7, 14 or 30 days in a row to mint a soulbound badge that stays with your Agent ID forever.

Points and badges are reputation records only. They are not tokens, cannot be traded, and have no monetary value. StreakID does not promise any return. Contract: view on BscScan · open-source code
